## 3A. Import Assessment

### Import Financing Needs
**Question:** What type of import financing do you need?

#### Pre-payment Financing
- **Do you need financing to pay suppliers before goods arrive?**
  - YES → **Products:** Import P/N, Import P/N under Open Account
  - Follow-up Questions:
    - Expected monthly/annual volume?
    - Can you provide collateral/guarantees?
    - Do you have established supplier relationships?

#### Document-Based Payments
- **Do you prefer document-controlled transactions?**
  - YES → **Products:** Import P/N under Bill for Collection, Letter of Credit Issuance
  - Follow-up Questions:
    - Are you comfortable with documents-against-payment terms?
    - Do you need payment guarantees to suppliers?
    - What is your credit line requirement?

#### Trust Receipt Arrangements
- **Do you need goods release before payment?**
  - YES → **Products:** Trust Receipt under Letter of Credit
  - Follow-up Questions:
    - Can you provide adequate security?
    - Do you understand the liability implications?

→ Continue to [4. International Transfers](#4-international-transfers)

## 3B. Export Assessment

### Export Financing & Risk Management
**Question:** What export support do you need?

#### Pre-shipment Financing
- **Do you need financing before shipment?**
  - YES → **Products:** Packing Credit (oa_pc, cb_pc, lc_pc)
  - Follow-up Questions:
    - Do you have confirmed export orders?
    - Can you provide inventory as collateral?

#### Payment Collection
- **How do you prefer to collect payments?**
  - **Documentary Collections** → **Products:** Outward Bill for Collection, Bill Receivable under Outward Bill for Collection
  - **Letter of Credit** → **Products:** Export Bill under Letter of Credit, Bill Receivable under Letter of Credit
  - **Open Account** → **Products:** Export P/N under Open Account

#### Additional Services
- **Do you need additional export services?**
  - **LC Proceeds Assignment** → **Products:** Assignment of Proceeds under Letter of Credit
  - **Shipping Documentation** → **Products:** Shipping Guarantee Issuance, Delivery Order Endorsement

→ Continue to [4. International Transfers](#4-international-transfers)

## 7. Risk Management Products

### Interest Rate & Currency Risk
**Question:** What risk exposures do you need to manage?

#### Interest Rate Risk
- **Do you have significant interest rate exposure?**
  - YES → **Products:** Interest Rate Swap
  - **QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:**
    - Sophisticated understanding of swap mechanics?
    - Adequate risk management policies?
    - Strong credit rating and financial strength?

#### Currency & Interest Rate Risk
- **Do you have both currency and interest rate exposures?**
  - YES → **Products:** Cross Currency Swap
  - **QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:**
    - Understanding of cross-currency swap complexity?
    - Ability to meet margin and collateral requirements?

## 9. Final Qualification Assessment

### Universal Suitability Check
Before recommending any products, ensure the client meets these requirements:

1. **Regulatory Compliance**: Compliant with all applicable regulations?
2. **Financial Strength**: Can provide recent financial statements and credit references?
3. **Business Purpose**: Legitimate business purpose for each product?
4. **Experience Level**: Appropriate experience with similar financial products?
5. **Risk Understanding**: Clear understanding of product risks?
6. **Documentation**: Ability to provide required documentation and legal agreements?
7. **Ongoing Relationship**: Seeking long-term banking relationship?
8. **Volume Commitment**: Clear expectations for transaction volume and frequency?

## Product Recommendation Summary

Based on the decision tree path, compile recommended products and present them with:

- **Primary Products**: Core products that directly meet client needs
- **Supporting Products**: Complementary products that enhance the primary products
- **Risk Management Products**: Products to mitigate identified risks
- **Next Steps**: Documentation and approval requirements
- **Ongoing Monitoring**: Regular review requirements for complex products

# Banking Products Catalog

## 1. International Outward Fund Transfer
- **Description**: Service enabling customers to transfer funds internationally to beneficiaries in foreign countries through various payment networks and correspondent banking relationships.
- **Tags**: remittance, international, wire transfer, SWIFT, cross-border payments
- **Segmentation**: Retail, SME, Corporate
- **Product Family**: Payment Services
- **Product Risk**: Medium (AML/KYC compliance, sanctions screening, operational risk)
- **Use Cases**: Business payments to suppliers, personal remittances, education fees, medical expenses abroad

## 2. Import P/N (Promissory Note)
- **Description**: Short-term financing instrument for importers to pay suppliers while awaiting delivery of goods, backed by import documents.
- **Tags**: trade finance, import financing, short-term credit, promissory note
- **Segmentation**: SME, Corporate
- **Product Family**: Trade Finance
- **Product Risk**: High (credit risk, country risk, operational risk)
- **Use Cases**: Import financing, working capital for importers, supplier payment facilitation

## 3. Import P/N under Open Account
- **Description**: Import financing through promissory notes for open account transactions where goods are shipped and delivered before payment is due.
- **Tags**: trade finance, import financing, open account, unsecured credit
- **Segmentation**: SME, Corporate
- **Product Family**: Trade Finance
- **Product Risk**: Very High (credit risk, no collateral, reliance on buyer's creditworthiness)
- **Use Cases**: Trust-based import transactions, established supplier relationships, working capital optimization

## 4. Export P/N under Open Account

- **Segmentation**: SME, Corporate

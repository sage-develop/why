## Things we want to know about the client
The following are some of the information we want to know about
the client:

1. Type of business (manufacturer, trader, services provider, ...)
2. Industry (e.g. automobile, healthcare, E&E, ...)
3. Size of business/segmentation (e.g. large corporate, middle market, sme, ...)
4. Coverage of business (i.e. domestic, cross-border, both)
5. Trading terms (i.e. credit terms, how many days)
6. Trading currencies
7. Cash flow position, ie. dependent on credit working capital financing?
8. Capex frequencies?

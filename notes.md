## Things we want to know about the client
The following are some of the information we want to know about
the client:

### 1. Type of business
**Why it matters:** Different business types have distinct financial needs, risk profiles, and growth patterns that require tailored banking solutions.

#### **1.1 Manufacturing & Production**
- **Manufacturers:** Working capital for raw materials, equipment financing, supply chain financing, export financing
- **Food & Beverage Producers:** Cold storage financing, seasonal working capital, food safety compliance
- **Plantation & Agriculture:** Seasonal financing, commodity hedging, crop insurance, equipment leasing, land development loans
- **Mining & Extraction:** Project financing, equipment leasing, environmental bonds, commodity price hedging

#### **1.2 Trading & Distribution**
- **Import/Export Traders:** Trade finance (LC, collections), FX hedging, multi-currency accounts, supply chain financing
- **Wholesalers/Distributors:** Inventory financing, logistics financing, supplier financing programs
- **E-commerce Platforms:** Payment gateway solutions, marketplace financing, digital banking services, cross-border payments
- **Retail Businesses:** Point-of-sale financing, inventory management, seasonal credit, franchise financing

#### **1.3 Services & Professional**
- **Professional Services** (consulting, legal, accounting): Cash flow management, professional indemnity insurance, expansion financing
- **Healthcare Providers:** Equipment financing, practice acquisition loans, medical insurance products, patient financing programs
- **Educational Institutions:** Infrastructure financing, student loan programs, endowment management, government grant management
- **Technology & Software Companies:** R&D financing, IP monetization, venture debt, foreign expansion funding

#### **1.4 Infrastructure & Development**
- **Construction Companies:** Performance bonds, project financing, equipment leasing, progress payment facilities
- **Property Developers:** Development financing, bridging loans, sales proceeds management, joint venture financing
- **Logistics & Transportation:** Fleet financing, fuel hedging, cargo insurance, cross-border documentation support
- **Energy & Utilities:** Project financing, green financing, regulatory compliance support, infrastructure development loans

#### **1.5 Consumer-Facing Businesses**  
- **Hospitality & Tourism:** Seasonal financing, renovation loans, travel insurance products, foreign exchange for international guests
- **Media & Entertainment:** Project financing for productions, equipment leasing, IP financing, royalty management
- **Franchisees:** Franchise financing, multi-location management accounts, standardized reporting solutions

#### **1.6 Emerging & Specialized**
- **Startups & Scale-ups:** Venture debt, growth financing, equity bridge financing, banking-as-a-service solutions
- **Digital/Fintech Companies:** Regulatory compliance support, payment processing, API banking solutions
- **Social Enterprises:** Impact financing, grant management, mission-aligned investment products
- **Family Offices:** Wealth management, multi-generational planning, investment services, private banking

**Key details to gather**
- **Primary business activities and revenue streams**
  - Core products/services offered
  - Revenue diversification across different streams
  - Recurring vs. project-based revenue patterns
  
- **Business model characteristics**
  - B2B, B2C, B2G customer mix and percentages
  - Direct sales vs. channel/distributor model
  - Subscription, transactional, or asset-based revenue model
  
- **Value chain and market position**
  - Position in industry value chain (upstream, midstream, downstream)
  - Market share and competitive positioning
  - Supplier and customer concentration risks
  
- **Operational patterns and cycles**
  - Seasonal patterns and cyclical nature of business
  - Production/service delivery cycles
  - Peak working capital requirements timing
  
- **Growth stage and trajectory**
  - Startup, growth, mature, or declining phase
  - Expansion plans (geographic, product, market)
  - Digital transformation initiatives and technology adoption

  - Market consolidation trends and competitive landscape
  
- **Financial characteristics and risk factors**
  - Typical working capital cycles and cash flow patterns
  - Capital intensity and equipment replacement cycles
  - Commodity price exposure and hedging needs
  - Customer concentration and credit risks
  
- **Growth drivers and investment needs**
  - Technology adoption and digital transformation requirements
  - ESG/sustainability compliance and investment needs
  - International expansion opportunities and challenges
  - M&A activity and consolidation trends

### 3. Size of business
**Why it matters:** Determines appropriate product complexity, pricing, service level, and decision-making processes.

#### **3.1 Enterprise & Large Corporate**
- **3.1.1 Global Enterprise (>$5B revenue, >10,000 employees):** 
  - Investment banking services, complex derivatives, multi-currency treasury management
  - Global cash pooling, supply chain financing, ESG financing solutions
  - Dedicated relationship teams, bespoke product structuring
  
- **3.1.2 Large Corporate ($1B-5B revenue, 2,500-10,000 employees):**
  - Sophisticated treasury solutions, risk management products, M&A advisory
  - Multi-market operations support, regulatory capital optimization
  - Senior relationship management, customized reporting

- **3.1.3 Corporate ($500M-1B revenue, 1,000-2,500 employees):**
  - Advanced cash management, hedging products, growth financing
  - International expansion support, acquisition financing
  - Relationship management with specialized product expertise

#### **3.2 Middle Market**
- **3.2.1 Upper Middle Market ($250M-500M revenue, 500-1,000 employees):**
  - Growth capital, acquisition financing, cash management optimization
  - Risk management education and products, international banking
  - Relationship banking with product specialists

- **3.2.2 Core Middle Market ($100M-250M revenue, 200-500 employees):**
  - Working capital optimization, equipment financing, expansion loans
  - Basic hedging products, multi-location banking, business advisory
  - Relationship management with periodic reviews

- **3.2.3 Lower Middle Market ($50M-100M revenue, 100-200 employees):**
  - Growth financing, asset-based lending, cash flow management
  - Simple FX products, basic treasury services, succession planning
  - Relationship banking with standardized products

#### **3.3 Small Business & SME**
- **3.3.1 Small Business Plus ($10M-50M revenue, 25-100 employees):**
  - Business loans, equipment financing, merchant services
  - Payroll services, basic cash management, business credit cards
  - Business banking specialists, digital solutions

- **3.3.2 Small Business ($2M-10M revenue, 10-25 employees):**
  - Operating loans, SBA financing, basic business accounts
  - Digital banking solutions, payment processing, business advisory
  - Branch-based or digital relationship management

- **3.3.3 Micro Business ($500K-2M revenue, 1-10 employees):**
  - Microloans, business credit cards, basic business checking
  - Digital-first solutions, automated underwriting, self-service options
  - Digital channels with limited personal interaction

#### **3.4 Startups & Emerging Growth**
- **3.4.1 Scale-up Companies ($5M-50M revenue, high growth trajectory):**
  - Venture debt, working capital lines, international expansion support
  - Banking-as-a-service, digital treasury, investor services
  - Specialized growth company banking teams

- **3.4.2 Growth Stage Startups ($1M-5M revenue, rapid scaling):**
  - Growth financing, equipment leasing, payroll financing
  - Digital banking, expense management, founder services
  - Technology-focused relationship management

- **3.4.3 Early Stage Startups (<$1M revenue, pre-revenue to early revenue):**
  - Founder banking, basic business accounts, credit building
  - Digital solutions, integrated fintech partnerships
  - Self-service digital platforms with advisory support

#### **3.5 Specialized Segments**
- **3.5.1 Family Offices & High Net Worth Entities:**
  - Private banking, investment management, multi-generational planning
  - Concierge services, alternative investments, tax optimization
  - Dedicated private banking teams

- **3.5.2 Professional Practices ($1M-20M revenue):**
  - Practice financing, equipment leasing, professional liability support
  - Succession planning, retirement plan services, personal banking integration
  - Industry-specialized relationship teams

- **3.5.3 Franchise Operations (varies by unit count and revenue):**
  - Multi-unit financing, franchise development loans, standardized reporting
  - Franchisor banking programs, territory expansion financing
  - Franchise-specialized banking solutions

**Key details to gather**
- **Financial metrics and scale**
  - Annual revenue/turnover (last 3 years and projections)
  - Number of employees (FTE and contractors)
  - Asset base and working capital requirements
  - EBITDA margins and profitability trends
  
- **Operational complexity**
  - Number of locations/facilities
  - Geographic footprint (local, regional, national, international)
  - Business line diversity and revenue concentration
  - Organizational structure complexity
  
- **Growth characteristics**
  - Historical growth rates and growth trajectory
  - Expansion plans (organic vs. acquisition)
  - Funding requirements and capital structure
  - Market position and competitive landscape
  
- **Decision-making and sophistication**
  - Management team experience and background
  - Board composition and governance structure
  - Financial management sophistication level
  - Technology adoption and digital maturity
  
- **Banking relationship complexity**
  - Current banking relationships and satisfaction
  - Product usage patterns and needs
  - Service level expectations and preferences
  - Risk tolerance and regulatory requirements

### 4. Coverage of business
**Why it matters:** Determines need for international banking services and foreign exchange solutions.
- **Domestic only:** Local currency solutions, domestic payment systems, local regulatory compliance
- **Cross-border:** FX hedging, trade finance, correspondent banking, multi-currency accounts
- **Both:** Comprehensive international banking platform, supply chain financing across borders

**Key details to gather:**
- Export/import percentages of total business
- Key trading countries and regions
- Future expansion plans
- Regulatory restrictions on international operations
- Currency exposure and hedging practices

### 5. Trading terms (i.e. credit terms, how many days)
**Why it matters:** Impacts working capital needs and appropriate financing solutions.
- **Extended credit terms (60-90+ days):** Higher working capital financing needs, receivables financing
- **Cash on delivery:** Lower financing needs, focus on operational efficiency
- **Advance payments:** Negative working capital, investment solutions for excess cash

**Key details to gather:**
- Average payment terms with customers
- Average payment terms with suppliers
- Cash conversion cycle duration
- Payment methods preferred by customers
- Credit control processes and bad debt experience

### 6. Trading currencies
**Why it matters:** Determines foreign exchange risk exposure and hedging requirements.
- **Single currency:** Minimal FX risk, focus on domestic solutions
- **Multiple currencies:** FX hedging products, multi-currency accounts, natural hedging opportunities
- **Volatile currencies:** Enhanced hedging strategies, currency risk management education

**Key details to gather:**
- Primary trading currencies and percentages
- Natural hedging opportunities (offsetting exposures)
- Current hedging practices and policies
- FX risk tolerance and management sophistication
- Future currency exposure plans

### 7. Cash flow position, ie. dependent on credit working capital financing?
**Why it matters:** Determines financing needs and appropriate credit facilities.
- **Cash generative:** Investment products, surplus cash management, expansion financing
- **Working capital dependent:** Revolving credit facilities, supply chain financing, factoring
- **Seasonal variations:** Flexible credit arrangements, seasonal financing solutions

**Key details to gather:**
- Monthly cash flow patterns and seasonality
- Working capital requirements and funding gaps
- Existing credit facilities and utilization
- Cash management practices and treasury function maturity
- Investment of surplus cash practices

### 8. Capex frequencies?
**Why it matters:** Determines need for term financing and equipment financing solutions.
- **Regular capex:** Equipment financing, lease solutions, technology upgrade financing
- **Lumpy capex:** Project financing, term loans, development financing
- **Minimal capex:** Focus on working capital and operational solutions

**Key details to gather:**
- Historical capex spending patterns
- Upcoming major capital projects or investments
- Asset replacement cycles
- Technology upgrade requirements
- Expansion or modernization plans
- Preferred financing methods for capital investments

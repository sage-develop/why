New Trade Collection Compatible Products

#### Buyer

#### BA - Bankers Acceptance

- A financing for Buyers (benefits both buyer and seller) (Usance LC, D/A Collection)
- In LC, the buyer can only use it if the payment term is Usance LC.
- In Trade Collection, can only be used if the seller created a time draft and the buyer’s bank accepted it.
- Seller gets bank guarantee (safer than buyer's promise)
- Use when the seller can discount BA for immediate cash (don't wait 90 days)
- If the seller doesn't want immediate cash, the seller still has a bank guaranteed maturity payment.
- Use when the seller wants stronger payment security than just LC issuing bank
- Use when the seller still considering if they want immediate cash or want to wait for LC maturity
- Use when the seller is concerned about waiting 90 days for payment from the buyer.

#### Seller - Post-shipment

#### DRF - Direct Recourse Financing

A post-shipment financing for Sellers.

- Seller gets cash, but if the buyer doesn’t pay, the bank advances funds to the seller. If the buyer doesn’t pay, the seller must refund the bank.
- Use this product if the seller needs advance fund and trust that the buyer can pay at maturity.
- Bank gives the seller money in advance.
- If the buyer doesn’t pay, the seller must refund the bank.
- Get the money now but if the buyer doesn’t pay, the risk returns to the seller.

#### OBP - Outward Bill Purchased

A post-shipment financing for Sellers

- Use when the seller wants immediate cash instead of waiting 30-60 days for the buyer to pay.
- Use when the seller wants the bank to handle buyer payment risk.
- The seller gets cash, no further obligation
- No further obligation for the seller after use this product.

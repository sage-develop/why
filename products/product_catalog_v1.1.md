# Product Catalog V1.1 - Index

This product catalog has been split into individual files for better organization and management. Each category now has its own dedicated file with all product details.

## Product Categories

### [Trade Finance Products](./trade_finance_products.md)
Complete catalog of trade finance products including import/export financing, letters of credit, collections, and related services.
- 22 products covering pre-shipment and post-shipment financing
- Import and export promissory notes
- Letters of credit services
- Bill collection services
- Trade-related guarantees

### [Cash Management](./cash_management.md)
Payment services, fund transfers, and liquidity management solutions.
- 7 products covering international and domestic payments
- Foreign currency accounts
- Liquidity management services
- Reconciliation services

### [Global Markets](./global_markets.md)
Foreign exchange and derivatives products for hedging and trading.
- 10 products covering FX spot, forwards, and options
- Interest rate and cross-currency swaps
- Treasury and hedging solutions

### [Loans](./loans.md)
Traditional lending products for working capital and capital expenditure financing.
- 3 core loan products: Term Loans, Revolving Credit, Overdrafts
- Both local and foreign currency facilities
- Collateralized lending solutions

### [Insurance](./insurance.md)
Risk management and insurance products.
- *Currently under development*

### [Investments](./investments.md)
Investment and wealth management products.
- *Currently under development*

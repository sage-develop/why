#### BA - Bankers Acceptance

- A financing for Buyers (benefits both buyer and seller)

- In LC, the buyer can only use it if the payment term is Usance LC.
- The buyer triggers the product but the seller drives the decision based on their needs.
- In Trade Collection, can only be used if the seller created a time draft and the buyer’s bank accepted it.
- Seller gets bank guarantee (safer than buyer's promise)
- When use, the seller can discount BA for immediate cash (don't wait 90 days)
- If the seller doesn't want immediate cash, the seller still has a bank guaranteed maturity payment.
- Use when the seller wants stronger payment security than just LC issuing bank.
- Use when the seller wants guaranteed payment certainty after documents are accepted.
- Use when the seller is concerned about waiting 90 days for payment from the buyer.
- Using Bankers Acceptance, means the seller can decide if they want immediate cash or want to wait for LC maturity.
- The buyer reimburses their bank at maturity.

#### Seller - Pre-shipment

#### Back-to-Back LC

- A financing/guarantee for Sellers who act as middlemen who don't manufacture. Pre-shipment timing
- Seller uses the original LC they agreed with the buyer as security to get the bank to issue another LC to the supplier.
- Use when original LC is NOT transferable.
- Use when the Seller needs more control.
- Use when the LC terms need to be different (amounts, dates, documents)

#### ECR - Export Credit Refinancing Pre Ship

- LC/Collection/OA Sellers pre-shipment

- A financing for sellers/exporters to produce goods before shipping, backed by export orders.
- This is a local government policy/scheme to encourage/grow local companies to export by providing low interest rate loans.

- Central bank provides low interest rate loans to companies that export "local content" via local banks.

#### Seller - Post-shipment

#### Export Credit Refinancing Post

- LC/Collection/OA Seller post-shipment

- A financing for sellers/exporters provided after goods are shipped and documents are submitted.
- This is a local government policy/scheme to encourage/grow local companies to export by providing low interest rate loans.

- Central bank provides low interest rate loans to companies that export "local content" via local banks.
- Provided after goods are shipped and documents are submitted.

# Trade Finance Products v3

## Letter of Credit

### Sellers - Pre-shipment

#### 2. Letter of Credit Advising

This is a pre-shipment service for Sellers

- This is the base LC product for sellers in **international and domestic** trade.
- Use when the buyer and seller have agreed to settle the trade using a Letter of Credit.
- Use when the seller wants to receive the LC through their bank.
- Use when the seller wants the advising bank to verify the **LC’s authenticity** and ensure the terms received are accurate.
- Required before the seller can access other LC products such as Letter of Credit Confirmation, or Trust Receipt Loan.

#### 3. Letter of Credit Confirmation

This is a pre-shipment service for sellers

- Use when the buyer and seller have agreed to settle the trade using a Letter of Credit.
- Confirmation can only be added as an add-on after Letter of Credit Advising.
- Confirmation increases cost because the seller’s bank takes on additional risk.
- Use when the seller values security of payment over cost savings.
- Applicable to both **international and domestic** trade.
- In domestic trade, use when the seller doesn’t trust buyer’s bank and wants to transfer **counter party risk** to their own bank
- In international trade, use when the seller wants to transfer **counter party sovereign risk** to their local bank.
- Use when trading with new buyers, unfamiliar partners, or higher-risk countries.
- Use when the seller wants to add assurance of payment by to rely on a domestic/local bank instead of the issuing bank alone.
- After selecting this product, the seller can also continue to choose others.

#### 4. Assignment of Proceeds under Letter of Credit

This is pre-shipment service for sellers

- Use when the buyer and seller have agreed to settle the trade using a Letter of Credit.
- Assignment can be done whether or not the LC is transferable.
- Assignment only passes on the right to receive money under the LC; the seller still must fulfill all LC terms and present compliant documents.
- Use when the seller wants to **redirect payments** to third parties involved in fulfilling the order.
- Use when the seller wants to repay the bank directly from LC proceeds.
- Simplifies settlement by allowing funds to go directly to the intended party instead of passing through the seller.
- After selecting this product, the seller can also continue to choose others.

#### 5. Letter of Credit Transferring

For the Seller or trading company that acts as a **middleman** and doesn't actually make the goods.

- Available only when the buyer and seller agree to settle trade using a **transferable Letter of Credit.**
- Allows the seller to avoid using their own cash to pay the supplier upfront.
- Use when the seller wants to secure a profit margin by transferring a smaller amount to the supplier.
- Suitable for middlemen who do not manufacture or ship goods but want to manage the trade flow and maintain control.
- If the LC is not transferable, the seller must use alternatives such as Assignment of Proceeds under Letter of Credit or paying the supplier directly.

#### 16. Packing Credit for Exporters

This is a pre-shipment loan for sellers

- This product is widely used as short-term working capital.
- Provides sellers with an upfront loan to prepare and ship goods.
- The loan is repaid once the seller receives payment for the goods.
- The bank takes the LC (or equivalent trade documents) as security.
- Use when the seller needs **pre-shipment working capital** to buy raw materials, cover production costs, packaging, or shipment.
- Useful for large orders, urgent shipments, or when the exporter’s cash flow is insufficient.
- Enables exporters to take on new orders without waiting for previous payments.
- Packing Credit requires strong collateral/security.
- After selecting this product, the seller can also use other LC products, including post-shipment options.

### Sellers - Post-shipment

#### 8. Export Bill under Letter of Credit

This is a post-shipment service for sellers

- Use when the buyer and seller have agreed to settle the trade using a **Sight LC**.
- The loan is used when the seller wants **immediate payment** under a Sight LC.
- The bank advances funds once compliant documents are presented.
- Loan type: advance/negotiation under Sight LC.
- Use when the seller does not want to wait for the buyer’s bank or buyer’s payment.

#### 9. Bill Receivable under Letter of Credit

This is a post-shipment loan for sellers

- Use when the buyer and seller agreed on **Usance LC**
- Provides financing immediately after documents are presented, even though payment is deferred until maturity.
- Loan type: discounting under Usance LC.
- Suitable when payment will only be made after a credit period (e.g., 30–180 days).
- Use when the seller does not want to wait months for the LC maturity to receive funds.
- Use when the seller wants **post-shipment working capital** to cover costs or accept new orders.

### Buyers - Pre-shipment

#### 1. Letter of Credit Issuance

This is a pre-shipment service for Buyers

- Use when the buyer and seller have agreed to settle the trade using a Letter of Credit.
- This is the base LC product for buyers in **international and domestic** trade.
- The issueing bank verifies the **LC’s authenticity** for the buyer.
- Required when the exporter demands a bank-backed guarantee of payment before shipping.
- Use when trading with unfamiliar partners or in higher-risk markets.
- Suitable for large deal sizes where the seller requires stronger payment assurance.
- Required before the buyer can access other LC products.

#### 6. P/N under Letter of Credit (Buyer)

This is financing for buyers.

- Available for both **international and domestic** trade.
- Use when **the B/L is issued in the buyer's name**, the goods legally belong to the buyer.
- The bank receives the B/L but cannot control the goods since title is already with the buyer.
- To obtain the B/L from the bank, the buyer signs a Promissory Note (P/N) — a written promise to repay later.
- Once the P/N is signed, the bank releases the B/L, and the buyer can collect the goods from the shipping company.
- Use when the buyer wants to free up cash flow instead of paying upfront at sight.
- Use when the buyer needs goods immediately to **sell goods first and repay later**.
- Use when the buyer wants to **extend credit terms beyond LC terms**.

#### 7. Trust Receipt Loan under Letter of Credit

This is financing for buyers.

- Use when (**the B/L is issued under the buyer’s bank’s name**) — meaning the bank initially owns the goods.
- The buyer’s bank releases the goods in trust after the buyer signs a Trust Receipt, promising to repay later.
- Used when the buyer needs immediate access to the goods to continue operations, sell to customers, or generate cash flow.
- Suitable when the buyer does not have enough cash at the time of goods arrival but can repay once the goods are sold.
- The buyer's business model depends on selling goods first to finance repayment.
- The buyer wants to **extend credit terms beyond LC terms**.
- The buyer wants to **sell goods first and pay later**.
- The B/L needs to arrive at the buyer's before using this product.

#### 18. Shipping Guarantee Issuance

This is a service for buyers.

- Use when the buyer and seller have agreed to settle the trade using a Letter of Credit.
- The B/L can be under the buyer’s bank or the buyer’s name in order to use Shipping Guarantee.
- Applies when goods have arrived but the buyer is still **missing the original B/L**.
- The buyer needs the goods immediately to avoid demurrage charges or supply chain delays.
- The buyer’s bank issues a guarantee to the shipping company so the goods can be released without presenting the B/L.
- The buyer must provide indemnity to their bank and pay fees until the original B/L is returned.
- When the original B/L arrives:
  - If issued in the buyer’s name, it is exchanged for the Shipping Guarantee.
  - If issued in the bank’s name, the buyer must also use Endorsement Services.

#### 19. Endorsement Services

This is a service for buyers.

- Use when the buyer and seller have agreed to settle the trade using a Letter of Credit.
- Use when **the B/L is issued under the buyer’s bank’s name**.
- The buyer’s bank endorses the B/L, transferring ownership of the goods to the buyer.
- Required for the buyer to take delivery when the B/L is not in their name.
- The original B/L must have arrived before this service can be used.
- Can be combined with a Trust Receipt Loan under Letter of Credit if the buyer needs the goods immediately but does not have the cash to pay upfront.
